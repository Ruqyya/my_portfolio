# 12 · Goddard palette (current direction)

Sampled from the Goddard Veterinary cover: navy `#0A2552`, ocean `#0F5974`, teal `#159797`, soft aqua highlight `#A0F2ED`, electric cyan `#20D6D2` for the heartbeat rule only. Light content ground `#F7FAFC`, stage `#E2EDF4`.

- Hero, case-study header, nav and contact use the same navy-to-ocean gradient with one teal bloom in the corner, away from text. Footer is deep navy.
- Case studies, projects, experience, FAQ and about sit on light surfaces; product screens sit on a pale stage.
- Contrast: navy ink on light 14.3:1; secondary text `#475569` 7.2:1; ocean `#0F5974` on light 7.4:1 (buttons, links); aqua on navy 11.7:1; light blue-grey `#C9D6E3` on navy 10.2:1, on the lightest ocean end 6.9:1. Teal `#159797` is 3.4:1 on light, so it is decoration only.
- Lighthouse: home 83 mobile / 95 desktop, Oladoc 85 / 99, accessibility 100, 0 contrast failures, CLS 0. Six pages checked at 390, 820 and 1440 px with no overflow or broken images.
- Note: this reverses the earlier "no dark hero" rule, at the owner's request.
